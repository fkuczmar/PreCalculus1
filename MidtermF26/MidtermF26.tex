Topics, with some suggested problems for review:

\emph{Note:} The scope of the exam will concentrate on the following topics and types of problems, but will not necessarily be limited to the problems listed below.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Circles, Types of Questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Find an equation of a circle given its center and radius or given its center and a point on the circle.

\item Explain the logic behind the equation of a circle.

\item Find an equation of the smallest circle through two given points.

\item Find an equation of another circle (not the smallest) through two given points.

\item Find equations of all circles with a given radius through two given points.

\item Find the coordinates of the points on a given circle closest to and farthest from a given point.
\end{enumerate}

\item \emph{Functions, Types of Questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Use function notation correctly.

\item Evaluate a function at a given input arithmetically and from a graph.

\item Solve equations with function notation algebraically and from a graph.

\item Translate between function notation and everyday English.

\item Find the domain and range of a function or relation.

\item A wide variety of applications.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Using point-slope to find an equation of a line given two points or a point and a slope.

\item Using point-slope to find an expression for a function in an applied scenario.

\item Using linear functions to answer related questions about inputs, outputs, and changes.
\end{enumerate}
