\emph{Important Note:} This exam assesses your mastery level of the methods and perspectives of \emph{our} class. Solutions using other methods, even if correct, will receive little or no credit.


Topics, with some suggested problems for review:

\emph{Note:} The scope of the exam will concentrate on the following topics and types of problems, but will not necessarily be limited to the problems listed below.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Circles, Types of Questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Find an equation of a circle given its center and radius or given its center and a point on the circle.

\item Explain the logic behind the equation of a circle.

\item Find an equation of the smallest circle through two given points.

\item Find an equation of another circle (not the smallest) through two given points.

\item Find equations of all circles with a given radius through two given points.

\item Find the coordinates of the points on a given circle closest to and farthest from a given point.
\end{enumerate} 

\item \emph{Circles, Some Practice Problems}

\begin{enumerate}
\item  Exercises 3, 5, 7, 11-13 of \emph{Section 5.1:Circles} of our OER text. See HW0, item 1a for the link.

\item Examples 1, 2, 3 and Questions 7, 11, 12, in the chapter \href{https://ximera.osu.edu/precalc1/PreCalculus1/Circles/Circles}{Circles, Part 1} of our class notes.

\item Questions 1-5 in the chapter \href{https://ximera.osu.edu/precalc1/PreCalculus1/CirclesCW1/CirclesCW1}{Circles, CW1} of our class notes. See the explanations to Questions 1, 3, and 4 in the chapter \href{https://ximera.osu.edu/precalc1/PreCalculus1/CirclesCW2/CirclesCW2}{Circles, CW2} for solutions to similar problems.

\end{enumerate}

\item \emph{Solving Quadratic Equations by Completing the Square}

\begin{enumerate}
\item See the chapter \href{https://ximera.osu.edu/precalc1/PreCalculus1/SolvingQuadraticEqs/SolvingQuadraticEqs}{Solving Quadratic Equations} of our class notes. You are responsible for all the material there.

\item Practice: Exercises 1-7 at the end of this chapter and many of the problems below about functions.
\end{enumerate}

\item \emph{Functions, Types of Questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Use function notation correctly.

\item Evaluate a function at a given input arithmetically and from a graph.

\item Solve equations with function notation algebraically and from a graph.

\item Translate between function notation and everyday English.

\item Find the domain and range of a function or relation.

\item A wide variety of applications.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Using point-slope to find an equation of a line given two points or a point and a slope.

\item Using point-slope to find an expression for a function in an applied scenario.

\item Using linear functions to answer related questions about inputs, outputs, and changes.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Directions}

\begin{itemize}
\item Arrive early. Students arriving after the first person leaves wil not be permitted to take the exam.

\item There is a 110-minute time limit for this test. Extra time will not be given if you arrive after the scheduled starting time.

\item No bathroom breaks. Once the exam begins, you will not be permitted to leave the room.

\item Bring a valid ID. See our Canvas Homepage or your Testing Center for options.

\item Bring pencils and erasers. Paper will be provided.

\item All other items are prohibited. This includes but is not limited to calculators, cell phones, notes, dictionaries. Failure to follow these directions on any part of the exam will result in a grade of 0 for the entire exam.

\item If you need scrap paper, request it from the proctor.

\item Use only the methods and perspectives of this class through HW4A. 

\item Your work will be evaluated based on the methods and perspectives of this class. Other methods and perspectives will receive little or no credit.

\item Do not use the quadratic formula. Do not use the slope-intercept form of a line.

\item Write a solution set when solving an equation.

\item Preface each computation block with a complete sentence describing what you are about to compute.

\item End each problem with a concluding sentence that both restates and answers the question.

\item Show all work. Do not skip steps.

\item Work vertically, with at most one equal sign per line. Do not split the page into columns.

\item Spread out your work. Leave plenty of space between lines.

\item Be neat. Write large.

\item Ask for extra paper if you run out of room.

\item You may {\bf not} ask any questions about this test. This includes all types of questions, including questions about meaning or asking for a translation.

\item Do {\bf not} share any information about this exam with your classmates.

\end{itemize}
