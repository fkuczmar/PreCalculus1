\begin{question} \label{Q0fe09efr}
The function 
\[
  g = f(v) = 40 - \frac{1}{25} \left( v - 50 \right)^2 \, , \, 25 \leq v \leq 65,
\]
expresses the gas mileage (in miles/gal) of a car in terms of its speed (in miles/hr). 

\begin{enumerate}
\item Find the gas mileage at a speed of $50$ miles/hour.

\item At what rate (in gal/mile) does the car burn gas at a speed of $50$ miles/hour?

\item At what speed(s) does the car burn gas at a rate of 0.04 gal/mile? Find the exact speed(s) without a calculator. Then approximate the speed(s) to the nearest tenth.

\item At what rate (in gal/hr) does the car burn gas at a speed of $50$ miles/hour?

\item At what speed(s) does the car burn gas at a rate of $\frac{5}{3}$ gal/hr?

\item Use the graph of the function $f$ and the slider $u$ (as need be) in Line 1 below to answer (exactly or approximately) the following questions. Explain your reasoning.

\begin{enumerate}
\item At what speeds does the car burns gas at the minimum and maximum rates (measured in gal/mile)?
\item  At what speeds does the car burns gas at the minimum and maximum rates (measured in gal/hour)?
\end{enumerate}
